# Workbench extension — frontend/index.html

Mode: Operate. Code-led extension of the user's existing cream/forest, two-column workbench. The user explicitly pins the palette, layout family, workflow and supplied expressions, asks for autonomous decisions and forbids approval pauses. This is not an open world/concept tournament; no random seed or generated comp is used.

THESIS: A quiet local archive desk with an expressive companion; reliable output and readable status lead.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream #f5f3e9, forest #234c3c, pale meadow #edf0e5, white paper #fffef9. System sans Chinese product typography, restrained Georgia italic for Paper. Three small terracotta mushrooms and actual supplied, hand-cropped colored raster stickers with a soft paper rim and offset shadow.

STORY: Enter course codes, choose quality and destination, connect to school, observe real work, collect verified PDFs. Unknown workload stays unknown. A pleased expression appears only when all courses are complete; waiting and errors stop looping motion.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand and concise course question, input/quality/directory/primary action on the left; a small companion forest scene and real stage/counter feedback on the right. Destination is visible before starting. Narrow viewports stack the same task and status, with automatic scroll to status on start.

FORM: Preserve the useful two-column workbench; one warm white composer and a pale meadow activity surface. 16px radii, 24–28px interiors, 10px control radii, clear focus. Seven supplied expressions replace Judy entirely. Three mushrooms, two tiny paper props. No external dynamic assets, heavy UI framework, mock business results or invented progress.

QUALITY BAR: Essential controls stay readable and discoverable; precise restrained motion, actual source expressions with clean colored edges, no page overflow at 320–1440px. Animation only for starting/active/complete, paused offscreen/hidden and removed under reduced motion. Test waiting/error/cancel/partial as first-class outcomes. Native folder settings affect real child-process destinations, persist atomically and preserve prior run paths.
